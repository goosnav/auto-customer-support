# Returns

For this fictional demonstration business, unopened books may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Contact support before returning an item; the owner must confirm the return instructions.

For a damaged or incorrect item, provide the order number and a description of the problem. A person reviews the case. The assistant cannot authorize a refund, approve an exception, change an order, or promise reimbursement.
