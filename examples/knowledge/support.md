# Getting a specific answer

The AI support assistant can explain approved business information. If a question needs order access, an exception, or information not in the approved knowledge, it records the conversation for a person to review.

For an order question, provide an order number and a contact email address or phone number. Never send a payment-card number, password, one-time login code, or other secret.

The example business has not promised a response-time deadline. An open ticket is a request for review, not confirmation that the problem is resolved or that an email has been sent.
